What Happens Next

After You Reach Out

Here's what to expect when you contact UHOA.

01

We Receive Your Message

All calls, emails, and form submissions go directly to our team. Nothing goes to an automated queue.

02

We Follow Up

We typically respond within 1–2 business days. If you called and left a voicemail, we'll call back as soon as possible.

Whether you need direct help, want to volunteer, or want to discuss a partnership — we'll match you with the right person and next step.
