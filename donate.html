Heroes Circle recognition is symbolic. No goods or services are provided in exchange for a gift. Gifts are tax-deductible to the extent permitted by law (IRC Section 170). Unsung Heroes of America is a 501(c)(3) public charity, EIN 47-3982494.

What you receive

Every Heroes Circle donor gets recognition that says what you did, and a clear record for your taxes.

A badge for your level

We email you a digital badge in your tier color after your first gift. Share it, and others may join you.

A certificate and a thank-you

You get a personal welcome from UHOA and a certificate that names your level.

A yearly giving statement

Monthly donors receive one statement each January that totals the year's gifts for your tax records.

Give in honor or memory of a veteran

A tribute gift honors someone who served, or remembers someone you lost, by helping a veteran who needs a home today. We can send a card to you or to the family so they know about the gift.

How a tribute gift works

  1. Make your gift online, by check, or by transfer.
  2. Email us the honoree's name, whether the gift is in honor or in memory, and where to send a card.
  3. We send your acknowledgment and the card.
