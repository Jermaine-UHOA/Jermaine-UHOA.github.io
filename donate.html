More Ways to Give

Other Ways to Support UHOA

Financial gifts are one part of how the community supports our veterans. Here are other meaningful ways to contribute.

👔

In-Kind Donations

Donate clothing (especially professional attire and warm weather gear), non-perishable food, hygiene products, and household items. Contact us to arrange a drop-off or pickup.

🏢

Corporate Giving

Businesses can partner with UHOA through corporate donations, employee giving campaigns, matching gift programs, or event sponsorships. We'll recognize your contribution publicly if you'd like.

📣

Spread the Word

Share UHOA with your network. Follow us on Instagram @UnsungHeroes.OfAmerica and tell your community about the work we do for veterans in Atlanta.

📅

Event Sponsorship

Sponsor one of our food drives, community events, or fundraising campaigns. Event sponsorships directly fund veteran services and put your brand in front of a caring community.

🎓

Grant & Foundation Funding

If you represent a foundation or grant-making organization, we welcome conversations about programmatic funding. Contact us to learn about our current priorities and data on veteran need in the Atlanta area.

⏰

Volunteer Your Time

Time is one of the most valuable gifts you can give. If donating money isn't possible right now, consider donating your hours. Learn about volunteer opportunities →

Why UHOA

Your Dollars Work Hard Here

UHOA is a lean, volunteer-driven organization. We don't spend your donations on unnecessary overhead. When you give to UHOA, the vast majority goes directly to programs: food, clothing, benefits navigation, job training support, and the time our team spends walking alongside veterans every day.
