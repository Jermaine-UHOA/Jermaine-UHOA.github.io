Every Purchase Houses a Veteran

Unsung Heroes of America merchandise is more than apparel — it is a visible stand for homeless and at-risk United States military veterans across the country. 100% of net proceeds from UHOA merchandise sales support our veteran housing, benefits navigation, and workforce preparation programs.

Our Program-Support Pledge

Unsung Heroes of America, Inc. — a Georgia-incorporated 501(c)(3) public charity (EIN 47-3982494) — commits that 100% of the net proceeds from every item sold through our official merchandise store directly supports our mission:

“To house homeless veterans and provide the support they need to reclaim stable, independent lives.”

“Net proceeds” means the amount remitted to UHOA after the on-demand print vendor’s product and fulfillment costs. UHOA does not retain any portion for staff compensation; all directors and officers of UHOA serve as uncompensated volunteers.

Where Your Purchase Goes

Your UHOA merchandise purchase helps fund:

  • Transitional housing operations — our planned DeKalb County / Decatur pilot site providing recovery-oriented, veteran-centered housing.
  • Case management and benefits navigation — one-on-one support helping veterans access VA disability, healthcare, education, and employment benefits they have earned.
  • Life-skills and workforce preparation — financial literacy, budgeting, cooking, interview preparation, and sustained employment support.
  • Food, clothing, and holiday distributions — direct material support for veteran families in metro Atlanta, including our annual Winter Warm-Up event.
  • Partner-landlord network development — expanding housing capacity through qualified Georgia landlord partners committed to housing veterans.

How Our Merchandise Program Works

UHOA sells apparel and other mission-aligned items through Bonfire, a B Corp-certified print-on-demand platform specifically built for nonprofit fundraising. Bonfire produces, packages, and ships every item on demand, so UHOA holds no inventory and incurs no upfront manufacturing cost. Net proceeds are remitted directly to UHOA on a recurring basis and tracked for inclusion in the organization’s annual financial reporting.

Merchandise sales are a supplementary fundraising channel for UHOA, not a primary activity. The majority of UHOA’s revenue comes from grants, individual donations, and corporate and foundation partnerships supporting our veteran housing programs.

Prefer to Give Directly?
