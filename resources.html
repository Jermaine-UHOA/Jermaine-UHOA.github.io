Veteran Resource Guide

The phone numbers, official links, and warnings every veteran and family member should have on hand. This page holds the things that do not change often. For time-sensitive news and weekly updates, follow our pages on social media.

🆘
If you or a veteran you know is in crisis right now:

Dial 988, then Press 1 · Text 838255 · Chat at veteranscrisisline.net
Free, confidential, and available 24 hours a day, 365 days a year, for veterans, service members, and their families.

Phone Numbers

Dial 988, then press 1
Text 838255 · Chat at veteranscrisisline.net
Free, confidential support, 24/7.

🏠

VA Homeless Veteran Resources

1-877-424-3838 (1-877-4AID-VET)
Call or chat for help if you are homeless or at risk of homelessness.

Any VA Question or Request

1-800-MyVA411 (1-800-698-2411)
The VA's main help line for benefits, health care, and requests.

1-877-927-8387
Community-based counseling and support for veterans and their families.

Source: U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs, va.gov/resources

Protect Your Benefits

Scammers Pose as the VA

The FTC and the VA warn that scammers go after veterans by phone, text, and direct message. Here is what they do, and none of it is real help.

🚫

Upfront Fees

Charging upfront fees to “help” with your claim is illegal.

🚫

A Promised 100% Rating

Promising you a 100% disability rating is a false promise.

🚫

Fast-Track Promises

Promising to fast-track your claim is also false.

✅
Questions about your claim?

Go straight to VA.gov or call 1-800-MyVA411 (1-800-698-2411). Full breakdown from the FTC: militaryconsumer.gov.
