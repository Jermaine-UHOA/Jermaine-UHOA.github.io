Leadership

Veteran-Founded, Volunteer-Led

UHOA was founded and is led by Jermaine Bullard Sr., a U.S. Army Staff Sergeant (E-6) who served three deployments to Iraq and two tours in Kuwait. He serves as Founder and Executive Director on a volunteer basis. Read the founder's story.

Because UHOA is volunteer-led and in its launch phase, we publish only figures we can document. As programs grow, this page will be updated with results, financial reports, and governance information.

Our Mission

To house homeless veterans and provide the support they need to reclaim stable, independent lives.

Our Vision

To increase the number of shelters dedicated to recovery-oriented care for veterans in Georgia.

What We Do

Our Work

Seven integrated service areas focused on stabilizing homeless and at-risk veterans in the Atlanta area.

🏠

Transitional Housing

Safe housing while veterans work through rehabilitation.

🌱

Recovery-Oriented Rehabilitation

Whole-person support built around each veteran.

📜

VA Benefits Navigation

Help claiming earned benefits, including Aid & Attendance.

📋

Life Skills

Budgeting, household management, and healthy routines.

💼

Workforce Training

Job training, placement, and retention support.

🍽️

Food & Clothing

Distribution events for immediate needs.
