The Decatur Veteran Recovery Home

A small, recovery-oriented transitional home for homeless veterans in DeKalb County.

The Need

A Place to Recover, Not Just a Place to Sleep

Many homeless veterans in metro Atlanta cycle between the street, short-term shelters, and crisis services without ever getting the stable footing recovery requires. Large shelters can be overwhelming for veterans carrying the invisible wounds of service.

UHOA's founder built this project around a simple idea: a small, intensely personal home where each veteran has a bed, a door that locks, and a team invested in what comes next.

Support the Project

🏠 Project Snapshot

  • Model: Small transitional, recovery-oriented home
  • Capacity: 6 to 12 beds
  • Location: Decatur, Georgia (DeKalb County)
  • Target opening: 2027
  • Who it serves: Homeless and at-risk veterans in the Atlanta area
  • Status: Launch phase
The Model

Housing Plus Support

Housing is the foundation. Every resident is connected to the services that make stability last.

🛏️

Safe Transitional Housing

A stable, dignified home removes the daily crisis of survival so recovery can begin.

📜

VA Benefits Navigation

Help claiming earned benefits, including HUD-VASH housing vouchers and Aid & Attendance.

💼

Job Training & Placement

Resume support, vocational guidance, and connections to veteran-friendly employers.

📋

Life Skills

Cooking, budgeting, household management, and healthy daily habits.

🤝

Individualized Plans

Each veteran gets a personal support plan built around their goals.

🏡

Path to Permanent Housing

Planning for long-term housing stability from the first week.
