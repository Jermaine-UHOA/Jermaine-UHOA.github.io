Our Purpose

Rebuilding Lives, One Veteran at a Time

Unsung Heroes of America was founded on a simple belief: the men and women who served our country should never have to fight alone when they come home. We meet veterans where they are — providing the comprehensive, individualized support they need to regain stability and rediscover purpose.

Based in Atlanta, Georgia, we work directly with homeless and at-risk veterans, connecting them with housing resources, employment opportunities, life skills training, and the VA benefits many didn't know they qualified for.

Our Full Story

Mission

To house homeless veterans and provide the support they need to reclaim stable, independent lives.

Vision

To increase the number of shelters dedicated to recovery-oriented care for veterans in Georgia.

🏅

100% of donations go directly to programs and services for veterans in need. We are a volunteer-driven organization committed to transparency and impact.

What We Do

Our Core Programs

We deliver a full spectrum of services tailored to each veteran's unique situation and needs.

🏠

Housing & Stability

We connect veterans with housing resources and advocate for transitional and permanent housing solutions to end homelessness.

💼

Job Training & Placement

Workplace training, resume support, and direct connections to employers who value the skills veterans bring to the workforce.

📋

Life Skills Training

Practical training in cooking, household management, budgeting, and healthy daily habits to support long-term independence.

🍽️

Food & Clothing Drives

Regular food and clothing distribution events throughout the Atlanta area to meet immediate needs in our veteran community.

📜

VA Benefits Advocacy

Many veterans don't know what they've earned. We help them access educational benefits, special pensions, healthcare, and more.
