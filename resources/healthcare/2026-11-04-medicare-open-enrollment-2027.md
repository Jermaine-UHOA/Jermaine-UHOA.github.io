# Medicare Open Enrollment runs through December 7 — compare 2027 plan choices

*Published: November 4, 2026 · Category: Healthcare · Source: U.S. Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS)*

**At a glance**

- **Who:** Veterans 65 or older, or under 65 with a qualifying disability, who are enrolled in or eligible for Medicare Parts A, B, C, or D.
- - **What:** The annual Medicare Open Enrollment Period to switch Medicare Advantage (Part C) and prescription drug (Part D) plans for the 2027 plan year.
  - - **How:** Log in at Medicare.gov/plan-compare, add your prescriptions, pharmacies, and providers, and compare plans before Sunday, December 7, 2026.
   
    - The Medicare Open Enrollment Period — set by 42 C.F.R. § 422.62(a) for Medicare Advantage and 42 C.F.R. § 423.38(b) for Part D prescription drug coverage — runs October 15 through December 7 every year, with coverage changes taking effect January 1. CMS reminds enrollees that plan formularies, premiums, and provider networks can change year to year, so even veterans satisfied with their current plan should check for 2027 changes before the window closes. Veterans enrolled in Medicare in addition to VA health care can keep both; Open Enrollment only governs the Medicare side.
   
    - **Key takeaway:** Log in at Medicare.gov/plan-compare before December 7 and compare at least one alternative plan against your current coverage. If your annual income is under $23,940 (individual) or $32,460 (married), also apply for Extra Help (Part D Low-Income Subsidy) through the Social Security Administration.
   
    - **Full details:** [Medicare Plan Compare — 2027 Plans](https://www.medicare.gov/plan-compare/)
