# Veterans Day 2026 — free meals and discounts for veterans on November 11

*Published: November 4, 2026 · Category: Benefits & Claims · Source: U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs (VA News)*

**At a glance**

- **Who:** Veterans and in many cases active-duty members and military retirees. Acceptable ID varies by business.
- - **What:** Annual Veterans Day savings — free meals and discounted services from participating national chains, plus year-round veteran discounts tracked by VA News.
  - - **How:** Carry proof of service, call ahead to confirm participation at local franchises, and check the VA News list for current national participants.
   
    - Veterans Day — observed November 11 and codified as a federal holiday at 5 U.S.C. § 6103(a) (renamed from Armistice Day by Pub. L. 83-380) — draws voluntary free-meal and discount offers from private-sector businesses nationwide. The VA News office publishes an annual round-up of participating national chains plus a companion list of year-round veteran discounts.
   
    - These offers are independent of VA benefits and vary by location: a national chain's participation does not guarantee every franchise participates, and ID requirements differ. Acceptable proof of service generally includes a DD-214, a Veteran Health Identification Card (VHIC), a Veteran designation on a state driver's license or ID, or a VA eligibility letter. Call the local business before you go.
   
    - **Key takeaway:** Carry proof of service on November 11 and check the VA News list before heading out — participation and ID rules vary by franchise.
