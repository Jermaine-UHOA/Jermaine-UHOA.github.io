# VA OSDBU NVSBE26 Training — free virtual sessions for veteran-owned small businesses on Nov 6 and Nov 10

*Published: November 4, 2026 · Category: Employment & Business · Source: VA Office of Small and Disadvantaged Business Utilization (OSDBU)*

**At a glance**

- **Who:** Veteran-owned and service-disabled veteran-owned small businesses (VOSBs and SDVOSBs), and veterans considering federal contracting.
- - **What:** Three free virtual training sessions preparing vendors for the 2026 National Veterans Small Business Engagement (NVSBE) and National Veterans Small Business Week (November 9–13, 2026).
  - - **How:** Register at the VA Webex links below. Attendance is free; sessions are virtual.
   
    - The VA Office of Small and Disadvantaged Business Utilization (OSDBU) — established under 38 U.S.C. § 8127 (the Veterans First Contracting Program) and 15 U.S.C. § 657f (the Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business Program) — is hosting three free virtual training sessions this week and next. The sessions prepare vendors for the 2026 National Veterans Small Business Engagement (NVSBE) and coincide with National Veterans Small Business Week, observed November 9–13, 2026.
   
    - **Session schedule (all times Eastern):**
   
    - 1. **Proposal Writing Success** — Friday, November 6, 1:00–2:00 PM ET. How VA evaluators actually score proposals and how to avoid the mistakes that cost good businesses the win. [Register](https://veteransaffairs.webex.com/weblink/register/r68113f4ce5bd80cfad16465fc13de85e)
      2. 2. **Engage the Right People** — Tuesday, November 10, 11:00 AM–12:30 PM ET. Identifying VA buyers, primes, and teaming partners worth your time; conversation and follow-up tactics. [Register](https://veteransaffairs.webex.com/weblink/register/rae3dcb2274b7adb9ab29ec6fb0bc6784)
         3. 3. **Understanding VA Contract Vehicles** — Tuesday, November 10, 1:00–2:00 PM ET. Using real VA award data to decide whether a contract vehicle is worth pursuing before investing resources in it. [Register](https://veteransaffairs.webex.com/weblink/register/rba92433d2e6cc00ed82907f3f81d03ee)
           
            4. **Key takeaway:** Veteran-owned small businesses that sell or plan to sell to the federal government should register for the Nov 6 proposal-writing session and the Nov 10 contract-vehicles session before attending NVSBE.
           
            5. **Full details:** [VA Office of Small and Disadvantaged Business Utilization](https://www.va.gov/osdbu/)
           
            6. For questions, contact VA OSDBU: DirectAccess@va.gov · (202) 461-4694.
