Our Standard

How We Decide What to List

A nonprofit's partners page is a promise: every organization listed is a relationship donors can call and verify. UHOA holds that standard tightly.

We list memberships we have formally joined, platforms that have formally verified us, program partnerships with active or completed commitments, and legal registrations we actively maintain. We do not list organizations we have only approached, applications under review, prospective supporters, or vendors we pay for services.

Where an organization appears on this page with its name only (no logo), that is intentional: use of a partner's logo requires their express written consent under their brand guidelines, and UHOA does not display any partner mark without that permission.

🎖️ Veteran-Led, Volunteer-Driven

UHOA was founded in 2016 by a U.S. Army combat veteran and remains volunteer-led. Every dollar raised and every hour of leadership time is directed toward launching and running our transitional housing program in Decatur, GA — not toward overhead.
