Make a Real Difference

Volunteer With Us

UHOA is a volunteer-driven organization. Every hour you give goes directly to changing a veteran's life. No experience required — just a heart to serve those who served.

Why It Matters

Your Time Has Real Impact

Unsung Heroes of America runs on the dedication of volunteers like you. There is no bureaucracy between your effort and a veteran's life changing. When you show up to help with a food drive, sit with a veteran to help them navigate their VA benefits, or use your professional skills to support our mission — you're the direct line between struggle and stability.

Veterans who've served in silence deserve to be seen. Being present — showing up consistently, listening, and advocating — is one of the most powerful things any volunteer can offer.

  • No military background required
  • Flexible scheduling — we work around your availability
  • Training provided for all roles
  • One-time or ongoing commitments welcome
  • Individual and group / corporate volunteer opportunities
100%

Volunteer-Driven

Every service UHOA provides is delivered by people who give their time freely. Your hours become a veteran's lifeline.

🏅

Corporate & group volunteering: We welcome teams from businesses, faith communities, universities, and civic organizations. Contact us to discuss a group volunteer day or ongoing partnership.

How You Can Help

Volunteer Roles

Whether you have a few hours or a standing weekly commitment, there's a meaningful role for you.

🍽️

Food & Clothing Distribution

Help sort, pack, and distribute food and clothing at our community drives throughout Atlanta. Great for individuals and groups of all sizes.

📋

Benefits Navigation Support

Assist veterans in gathering documents and understanding the VA benefits application process. Training provided — no prior VA knowledge required.

💼

Employment Mentorship

Use your professional background to help veterans with resume writing, interview prep, and career guidance. Available remotely or in-person.

🧑‍🍳

Life Skills Coaching

Lead workshops on cooking, budgeting, household management, or healthy habits. Share practical knowledge that builds lasting independence.

🚗

Transportation & Logistics

Help veterans get to VA appointments, job interviews, or program sessions. Even a single ride can change the outcome of someone's day.

📣

Outreach & Awareness

Help spread the word about UHOA and our programs — on social media, at community events, or within your own network. Awareness saves lives.

💻

Administrative & Tech Support

Lend your skills in data entry, communications, grant writing, graphic design, web, or other office tasks to help UHOA operate and grow.

🤝

Peer Support & Mentorship

Veterans who've overcome homelessness or other hardships are especially valuable mentors. If you've walked this road, consider sharing your experience to light the way for others.
